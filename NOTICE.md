# Learning content and attribution

The MIT license covers this application's original code. It does not relicense third-party audio or transcripts.

## ELLLO

Eight original MP3 recordings in `assets/listening/elllo-*.mp3` and their transcripts in `listening-recordings.js` are credited to Todd Beuckens / ELLLO (elllo.org). ELLLO states that students and teachers may download, copy and distribute its MP3s and text for educational purposes, excluding commercial transfer. These files and texts retain those conditions, not the MIT license. No specific Creative Commons license version is inferred.

- Permission statement: https://www.elllo.org/english/Mixers/
- Current usage information: https://www.elllo.org/about/
- Official lesson, media and speaker-information URLs are included with every item in `listening-recordings.js`.

Original audio is unaltered. Transcripts have minor spelling corrections. Approximate sentence timing and application-specific comprehension questions were added for this educational project. Speaker-origin labels follow the official course catalogue and do not certify a particular regional accent.

## Voice of America

The Bottom Line and On the Ball are VOA Learning English productions, credited to Voice of America / learningenglish.voanews.com. Audio was extracted from the original videos; captions were transcribed for learning. VOA identifies its own original material as public domain and requests source credit. Third-party agency or music content is not covered by that statement.

- Usage policy: https://learningenglish.voanews.com/p/6861.html
- Programme and media URLs: `listening-library.js`.

## British Council

Only titles, original descriptions written for this app, and links to the official lessons are provided. British Council audio, transcripts and exercises have not been republished.

- Terms: https://www.britishcouncil.org/terms

## AI audio

Daylight original exercises use DeepSeek Stella only when the user requests playback. They are identified as generated audio. Such audio is not presented as an authenticated regional-accent sample.
